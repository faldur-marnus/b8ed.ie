We ranked every way of thanking the bus driver and, frankly, you're doing it wrong

The mumbled "thanks" from the middle door does not count. It has never counted.

By The Thursday DeskThu 1 Oct, 10:00Bait rating 🎣🎣🎣🎣🎣

It is the great Irish ritual. You get to your stop, you get off, you thank the driver. But not all thank-yous are equal. Here's the definitive ranking.

  1. The full "thanks a million, driver", delivered at the front, with eye contact. Elite. You were raised right.
  2. The two-finger salute from the footpath, after the bus pulls off. Respect. Old-school.
  3. The "thanks, cheers", the hedge-your-bets of gratitude. Fine. Nothing more.
  4. The nod. Acceptable only if you're carrying shopping.
  5. The "thanks" shouted from the middle door. The driver can't hear you. You know the driver can't hear you. Who is this for?
  6. Saying nothing. We have your name. We have your address. We're telling your mother.

Disagree? Fair. But the ranking is final and the comments are where you'll be wrong.
