LIVE: Man attempts to get a pint and get back to his seat before the second half starts

Seán left his seat at 20:41 with a tenner, a dream and no real plan. We're following every step.

By The Wednesday DeskWed 7 Oct, 20:41Bait rating 🎣🎣🎣🎣🎣

Half-time at the stadium. One man, one mission, a queue that goes back to roughly 2019. Stay on this page. We mean it.

LIVENewest first · 9 updates
  1. 21:01

    SEÁN IS IN HIS SEAT. He has missed nothing, he insists. He has missed a goal. We'll leave it there for tonight. Same time Saturday.

  2. 20:59

    He is on the steps. He has spilled what experts are calling "a fair bit". He has said "sorry, sorry, sorry" 11 times to people in his row.

  3. 20:57

    Card tapped. Seán has two pints. The roar from inside suggests the teams are back out. He has to make it up 40 steps carrying €16-something of liquid. This is the moment.

  4. 20:55

    Seán is at the front. He orders two pints. The barman informs him the price. Seán is now looking at his tenner the way you'd look at a photo of a dead relative.

  5. 20:52

    UPDATE: Seán's brother has texted "where are u". Seán has replied "nearly there". He is not nearly there.

  6. 20:50

    A steward has announced the bar is "cash and card", which Seán has taken as a personal sign from God.

  7. 20:47

    Progress. Seán has advanced three people. One of them was just a man standing near the queue who has now been forced to join it out of politeness.

  8. 20:43

    Seán has reached the concourse. So has everyone else. He describes the queue as "grand" and "moving". It is neither.

  9. 20:41

    Whistle goes. Seán (34, Phibsboro) is out of his seat before the ref has the whistle out of his mouth. "Back in ten," he tells his brother. His brother does not look up.
