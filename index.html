What B8ED is

A satirical Irish media page that plays the engagement game and laughs at it.

B8ED covers sport, pop culture, politics and comedy in the quick, shareable style of Irish digital publishers such as Joe.ie.

  • Format
    Quick, shareable posts built for scrolling.
  • Voice
    Satire aimed at bait culture itself.
  • Output
    New content every day of the week.
  • Standards
    Everything published stays within Irish law.

Signature segments

  • Baited Fake News

    Satirical headlines and stories that parody the clickbait news cycle.

  • Rage Bait

    Deliberately provocative posts that poke fun at outrage culture.

  • Match-day vox pops

    Comedians interview fans before and after games.

  • Live game updates

    Real-time match coverage that keeps readers on the page.

  • Comedy night

    Clips and material from our monthly live night in Dublin.

How contributors work

Day owners

Each day of the week has an owner who shapes that day's content.

Anonymous by design

Contributors from other media circles can write freely, without the limits of their usual brand.

Growth through networks

An owner can post twice in a day or bring in people from their own circle.

Volunteer first

Early contributions are voluntary. Payment follows revenue, and day owners are planned to hold a stake.

Building an audience we own

Competitions grow the email list. The list gives us a direct line to readers.

Social platforms bring readers in. The list keeps them.
