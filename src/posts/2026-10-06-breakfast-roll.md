We need to talk about the breakfast roll.

Not as a food. As a decision. Because nobody, at any point in history, has ordered a breakfast roll while having a good day.

Look at what it is. It's a full Irish that's too embarrassed to sit down. It's a fry that doesn't want to make eye contact. It's six food groups stuffed into a baguette like commuters onto a Luas.

And the brown sauce versus red sauce debate? A distraction. The real question is why you're eating it in the car at 7:40am with the radio off, staring at a wall.

We're not saying don't have one. We're saying it's not a meal. It's a coping mechanism with a rasher in it.

Now go on. Tell us we're wrong. We'll be in the deli queue.
