We've done the research. By research, we mean we asked the group chat.

1. You've given directions using a pub that closed in 2003.
2. You call it "the Pigeon House" and refuse to elaborate.
3. You've said "I'm only around the corner" from Swords.
4. You know which side of the river you're from and think it matters. It does.
5. Every second person you meet is "a cousin of someone".
6. You've told a tourist the Spire was "for the millennium" with confidence.
7. You've been to Howth for chips and called it a holiday.
8. The 99 Flake is a seasonal religious observance.
9. You've waited for a bus for long enough to rethink your life.
10. You say "deadly" without irony.
11. You've walked down Grafton Street just to "see who's around".
12. Your granny can name every Dublin footballer since 1974.
13. You've said "the rent is mad" every week since you were 22.
14. You've bought a gaff-sized tin of Roses and then hidden it from your own family.
15. You know the exact moment a "quick one" became a session.
16. You've been to Funderland and come out a different person.
17. You've told someone from Cork that Dublin is "more chill". They did not agree.
18. Sunday dinner was at 1pm. Not 1:15. 1pm.
19. You've said "go way outta that" when someone complimented your jacket.
20. You've used the phrase "your one" about someone you've known for 15 years.
21. You read all 21 of these. We told you.

Ah here.
