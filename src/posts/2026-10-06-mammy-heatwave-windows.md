A Kildare mother has closed every window in the family home during an unseasonable October heatwave, citing the need to "keep the heat in".

The temperature inside the house had reached 31 degrees by lunchtime, according to a thermometer her son bought "specifically to win this argument".

"I don't want to hear it," said Bernadette, 63. "You open a window and you let the heat out, and then where are you? Cold. That's where."

When it was pointed out that being cold was, at that point, the goal, Bernadette responded by putting on the immersion "just for an hour, for the dishes".

Her husband, who has not been consulted on any household temperature decision since 1994, was found sitting in the car with the air conditioning on. "It's the only place I have," he said.

Met Éireann has not commented. Bernadette has, however, issued a warning that "it'll turn", and has brought the good coats down from the attic.
