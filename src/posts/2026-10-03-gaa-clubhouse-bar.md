Every club is different. Every club bar is exactly the same.

1. "Who has the keys?"
2. "That young lad will play for the county. Mark my words. He's eleven."
3. "Sure we'll have the one."
4. "Is there any chance of the heat being put on?"
5. "The lotto's up to €14,000. Fourteen thousand. Tell your mother."
6. "Ah that was never a free."
7. "I remember when this was all fields." (It is still all fields.)
8. "Fair play to the ladies who did the sandwiches."
9. "Right, the last one now." (It is not the last one now.)

Did we miss one? Of course we did. Send it to the group chat and tell them B8ED forgot the best one.
