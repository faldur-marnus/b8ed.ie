It's the fastest field sport in the world, and the oldest. Surely, we thought, the people walking up Jones' Road know at least one rule. We were half right.

> "You can't throw it. Well, you can, sort of. It depends who's reffing."
-- Mairéad, 52, Thurles

> "No pucking the ball into the crowd on purpose. I don't think that's written down but it's a rule."
-- Darragh, 23, Ballyhale

> "The rule is Kilkenny win."
-- A man from Clare who asked us not to use his name, then said his name into the microphone

> "Four steps. Or is that the football. It's four of something anyway."
-- Niamh, 31, Rathmines, at her first ever match

> "Never ask an Offaly man about 1998. He will tell you. For three hours. That's the first rule."
-- Paddy, 67, Birr

## The verdict

Not one fan got the same rule. Two of them argued about it for the length of the road. A third has since been in touch to say he's still thinking about it.
