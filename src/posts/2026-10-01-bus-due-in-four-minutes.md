A Dublin bus listed as "due in 4 minutes" on the real-time sign has now been due in 4 minutes for over two days, according to people who have not left the stop since.

"I came down Tuesday morning for a dentist appointment," said Gerry, 58. "Four minutes, it said. I thought, grand, I'll wait. That was Tuesday."

The group of commuters has since organised itself into what one member described as "a small society". A rota for the bench has been agreed, and a man from Cabra has been elected chairperson "mainly because he has a flask".

At one point on Wednesday the sign briefly changed to "due", causing scenes. It then changed back to "4 min".

"I'm not leaving now," said Gerry. "If I leave, it'll come. That's how it works. Everyone knows that."
