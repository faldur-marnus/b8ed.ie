"Grand" is the most important word in Hiberno-English and also the least useful. Here is what it actually means, depending on how it's said.

1. **"Grand!"** (bright, rising) Things are actually fine. Rare. Treasure it.
2. **"Grand, yeah."** Fine, but don't ask a follow-up.
3. **"Ah, grand."** Mildly disappointed but too polite to show it.
4. **"Grand so."** The conversation is over. Leave.
5. **"Yeah no, grand."** Something has gone wrong and you are partially to blame.
6. **"It'll be grand."** It will not be grand.
7. **"Sure it's grand."** We will never speak of this again.
8. **"Grand, grand, grand."** Panicking. Actively panicking.
9. **"I'm grand."** (said while bleeding) Needs stitches. Will not go to A&E.
10. **"Grand."** (flat, no eye contact) You're in serious trouble with your mother.
11. **"...grand."** (long pause before) Call an ambulance. Call a priest.

Hope that helps. If not, sure it's grand.
