A rural TD has replaced their weekly constituency clinic with a WhatsApp group, which now contains 4,312 unread messages, 600 voice notes and one photo of a pothole that has been shared 212 times.

"This is about access," the TD told B8ED. "In the old days you'd have to queue in a hotel function room on a Friday evening. Now you can send me a voice note about your hedge at 2am. That's progress."

Constituents say the group was working well "for about a day and a half", before it was taken over by a long-running dispute about a county council parking scheme and someone's nephew trying to sell a used Hiace.

The TD has admitted to muting the group "for a while", but insisted that a member of staff "reads every message". The member of staff is understood to be the TD's 15-year-old niece, who has been reacting to messages with a thumbs up since August.
