Somewhere along the line, Irish sport took a wrong turn. That turn was away from the orange.

Every adult who played underage sport in this country remembers it. A Tupperware box. Eight oranges cut into quarters. Someone's dad. The rind in your mouth like a gumshield. Pure performance.

Now? Electrolyte sachets. "Hydration strategies." A sports scientist with a clipboard. Did any of that ever win an U-12 B league in the rain? Didn't think so.

We're calling it. The orange did more for this country's sporting development than any high-performance centre.

Agree? Disagree? Typing a reply about vitamin C in capital letters? Good. That's the orange talking.
