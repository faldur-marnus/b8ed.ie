This one's not satire. We're starting a monthly comedy night in a Dublin bar, and we want you on it.

## How it works

**Headline names.** Comedians with big followings bring the first audience.

**Open mic.** New acts fill out the rest of the bill. We'll be watching, and the ones who land it get asked back.

**Comedy Friday.** Material from the night fills a full day of B8ED every Friday.

## From open mic to podcast

1. Perform at the monthly comedy night.
2. Host match-day vox pops for B8ED at grounds across Dublin.
3. Move on to podcast appearances, including on Féach podcasts.

Each step means more exposure and a bigger platform.

## Want a slot?

Email [hello@b8ed.ie](mailto:hello@b8ed.ie?subject=Open%20mic%20slot) with your name, a link to a clip if you have one (you don't need one), and how long you've been gigging. Venue and first date to be announced.

Run a bar that wants a packed room once a month? [We'd love to hear from you too](/advertise/#partner).
