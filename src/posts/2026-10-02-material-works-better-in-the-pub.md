A Dublin open mic comedian has said his debut five-minute set "absolutely kills" in the pub with his friends, and that the problem on the night was "the room".

Conor, 26, performed a set built around a story about his mate Dinny falling into a canal in 2019. "You'd want to have known Dinny," he said afterwards. "If you knew Dinny, that's the funniest thing you've ever heard."

The audience of 34 people did not know Dinny. Dinny was not in attendance.

"It's a tough room," said Conor, of a room that contained his mother, two cousins and a man who had come in to use the toilet.

Conor confirmed he would be back next month with "a whole new five", which sources close to the comedian say is the same five "but with more Dinny".
