Every year the Budget arrives, and every year the country asks one question: what's in it for me? We've read the whole thing so you don't have to. (We read the headlines.)

## The good news

You are getting more money. Roughly €3 a week. Possibly €3.50 if you're a widowed farmer with a heat pump.

## The other news

Everything costs more. Roughly €5 a week more. Possibly €7 if you drink, drive, smoke, eat, heat your home or go outside.

## So am I better off?

Depends who you ask. Ask the Government, yes. Ask the opposition, no. Ask your Da, "it's a disgrace", which he also said about the last one, and the one before.

## What should I do?

Give out about it. It won't change anything, but it's tradition, and the Budget is nothing if not traditional.
