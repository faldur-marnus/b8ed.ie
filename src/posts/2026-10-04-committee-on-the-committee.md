The Government has announced a new Oireachtas committee to examine why a previous committee took so long to deliver its findings.

The original committee, set up to look into delays in planning, delivered its report after six years, by which time the planning in question had been delayed further, partly by the committee.

"We take this very seriously," a Government spokesperson said. "Which is why we've set up a committee. That's what taking things seriously is."

Asked how long the new committee would take, the spokesperson said it would be "as long as it needs to be, and then a small bit longer to be safe".

A third committee, to monitor the progress of the second committee, is said to be "at an advanced stage of being thought about".

The opposition has called the plan "a farce" and announced it will be seeking seats on all three.
