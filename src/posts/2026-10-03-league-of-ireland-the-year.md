A League of Ireland supporter from Dublin has confirmed, for the 19th consecutive season, that this is the year.

Speaking from the same spot on the terrace he has stood in since 2007, Declan Furlong, 41, told B8ED that "all the signs are there".

"We've a new lad up front who's very good on the ball when there's nobody near him," he said. "And we've a manager who's tactically very, very angry. You can't coach that."

Asked what "the year" actually meant, Declan became evasive. "It means the year. You'll know it when you see it."

Declan's wife confirmed he had also said this was the year in 2011, 2014, 2016, 2019 and "during the pandemic, to the telly, when there were no fans allowed".

With the season ending this month, Declan said he was "already looking at next year, which, between ourselves, is the year".
