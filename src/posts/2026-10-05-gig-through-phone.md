A Dublin woman who paid €96 for a standing ticket at a sold-out gig has confirmed she watched the entire show through the phone of the man directly in front of her.

"From the second the lights went down, the phone went up," said Aoife, 29. "Every song. Every single song. Sometimes he'd zoom in. Sometimes he'd turn it around and do a selfie video, and then I'd get to see him for a bit, which was a change."

Aoife estimates she saw the actual stage for "around 40 seconds" during the encore, when the man's battery dropped to 2%.

The man in question, reached by B8ED, said he was recording "for the memories" and that he planned to watch the footage "never, probably".

Aoife said she'd since asked him to send her the videos. "If I'm going to have watched it on his phone, I may as well have it on mine," she said.
