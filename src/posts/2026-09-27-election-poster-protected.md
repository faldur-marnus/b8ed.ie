An election poster cable-tied to a lamppost in north Dublin since the 2024 local elections is set to be listed as a protected structure, B8ED understands.

The poster, now faded to the point where the candidate looks "like a ghost in a suit", has become a local landmark. Residents give directions using it. A tour bus stopped beside it last month.

"It's part of the streetscape now," said one neighbour. "Every morning I walk past and he's there, smiling at me. He didn't get elected, but he never left. There's something beautiful in that."

The candidate in question, contacted by B8ED, said they "fully meant to take it down" and "would get to it".

Council sources say that once listed, the poster cannot be removed, altered or replaced without planning permission, which, given recent timelines, should come through around 2034.
